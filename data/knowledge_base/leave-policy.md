# Paid Time Off and Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy describes every category of paid and unpaid leave available to
employees of Northwind Systems. It applies to all full-time and part-time
employees on a Northwind Systems payroll. Contractors and agency staff are
covered by their supplier agreement and are out of scope for this policy.

## 2. Annual leave (PTO)

Full-time employees are entitled to **24 working days of paid annual leave**
per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated entitlement based
on contracted hours.

- Annual leave **accrues at 2.0 days per completed month of service**.
- New joiners accrue from their start date; leave may be taken during
  probation with manager approval.
- The leave year runs from **1 January to 31 December**.
- A maximum of **5 unused days may be carried over** into the next leave year.
  Carried-over days **expire on 31 March**. Days beyond the 5-day cap are
  forfeited and are not paid out, except where local law requires otherwise.

## 3. Sick leave

- Employees receive **12 paid sick days per calendar year**. Sick days do not
  accrue and do not carry over.
- Absences of **1–2 consecutive days** require notification to the line manager
  before the start of the working day; no certificate is needed.
- Absences of **3 or more consecutive days** require a medical certificate
  uploaded to PeopleHub within 5 working days of returning.
- Sick leave beyond 12 days in a year moves to the long-term illness process
  described in Section 9.

## 4. How to request leave

1. Open **PeopleHub → Time Off → New Request**.
2. Select the leave type, dates and (optionally) a handover note.
3. Submit. The request routes automatically to your line manager.
4. You receive an email and PeopleHub notification when it is approved or
   declined.

**Notice periods**

| Length of absence | Minimum notice |
|---|---|
| 1–2 days | 2 working days |
| 3–9 days | 10 working days |
| 10+ days | 20 working days |

Managers must respond to a leave request within **3 working days**. If a
manager does not respond within 5 working days, the request escalates
automatically to the manager's manager.

## 5. Declined requests and appeals

A manager may decline leave only for a documented business reason, such as a
release freeze or minimum team coverage. The reason must be recorded in
PeopleHub. If you disagree with a decision, raise it with your HR Business
Partner within 10 working days; see the Grievance Procedure (HR-POL-010).

## 6. Public holidays

Public holidays are granted in addition to annual leave and follow the
calendar of your registered work location. Employees may swap up to **2 public
holidays per year** for days of personal or religious significance, with
manager approval. The authoritative list is the company holiday calendar in
PeopleHub.

## 7. Other paid leave types

| Leave type | Entitlement | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bereavement leave | 5 days (immediate family), 2 days (extended family) | No documentation required |
| Marriage leave | 3 days | Once per employment |
| Jury duty / civic duty | As summoned | Copy of summons required |
| Volunteering day | 1 day per year | Approved charitable activity |
| Study / exam leave | 5 days per year | Linked to an approved course (see HR-POL-012) |
| Compassionate / emergency dependant leave | Up to 3 days per event | For unforeseen dependant emergencies |

Parental, maternity, paternity, adoption and caregiver leave are covered by a
separate policy: **Parental and Family Leave (HR-POL-002)**.

## 8. Unpaid leave and sabbatical

Employees with **4 or more years of continuous service** may apply for an
unpaid sabbatical of 1 to 3 months. Applications go to your manager and HR
Business Partner at least **90 days** in advance. Benefits continue during a
sabbatical; annual leave does not accrue during unpaid periods.

## 9. Long-term illness

Absence beyond 12 consecutive sick days moves into the long-term illness
process: HR contacts the employee, occupational health support is offered, and
salary continuation follows the schedule in the Benefits policy (HR-POL-008).

## 10. Leave balances

Live balances are visible in **PeopleHub → Time Off → My Balance**, and can be
requested from the Enterprise Knowledge Copilot. Balances are recalculated
nightly. Discrepancies should be reported to `people-ops@northwind-example.com`
within 30 days.

## 11. Exceptions

Any exception to this policy requires written approval from the Head of People
Operations. Local statutory entitlements always take precedence where they are
more generous than this policy.
