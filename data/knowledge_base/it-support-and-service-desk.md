# IT Support and Service Desk Guide

## 1. How to get help

| Channel | Use it for | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| **ServiceDesk portal** | Any request or fault; creates a tracked ticket | 24×7 |
| `#it-help` chat channel | Quick questions, triage | 08:00–19:00 local, Mon–Fri |
| IT walk-up desk | Hardware swaps, in-person help | 09:00–17:00 at hub offices |
| Emergency IT phone line | Total loss of service, lost device | 24×7 |

Always raise a ticket, even after asking in chat — an untracked request has no
SLA.

## 2. Ticket priorities and SLAs

| Priority | Definition | First response | Target resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Cannot work at all; no workaround; or security exposure | 30 minutes | 4 business hours |
| P2 | Major impairment with a workaround | 2 business hours | 1 business day |
| P3 | Standard request (access, software, hardware) | 1 business day | 3 business days |
| P4 | Low-impact question or improvement | 2 business days | 10 business days |

SLA clocks run during local business hours except P1, which runs 24×7. Priority
is set by IT based on impact and urgency and can be challenged in the ticket.

## 3. Common requests

- **Password or MFA reset** — self-service in the identity portal; identity
  verification by video call is required if self-service fails.
- **New software** — request in ServiceDesk; approved catalogue items are
  installed automatically, anything else needs a security review (SEC-POL-011).
- **Access to a system** — ServiceDesk → Access Request, approved by the line
  manager and, for Restricted data, by the data owner.
- **Hardware refresh** — laptops are refreshed every 3 years, or earlier if
  faulty. Accessories are ordered from the standard catalogue.
- **Leaver access removal** — raised automatically by HR; managers should
  confirm handover of shared accounts.

## 4. Equipment standards

Engineering roles receive a 32 GB developer laptop; other roles receive the
16 GB standard model. Requests for non-standard equipment need a business
justification and department-head approval. Peripheral standards (monitor,
keyboard, headset) are listed in the IT catalogue and count against the home
office allowance for remote employees (HR-POL-005).

## 5. Escalating a ticket

If an SLA is missed, use the **Escalate** button in the ticket. This notifies
the IT duty lead. Repeated SLA breaches for the same service are reviewed in the
monthly IT service review. Production outages are not handled through
ServiceDesk escalation — declare an incident instead (OPS-POL-004).

## 6. Data and backups

Company data must be stored in approved cloud locations, not on local disks.
Approved storage is backed up continuously with a 35-day retention window.
Restores are requested through ServiceDesk with a P2 priority and target 1
business day.
