# Expense and Business Travel Policy

## 1. Principle

Spend company money as if it were your own. Expenses must be **necessary,
reasonable, and evidenced**. Where this policy is silent, ask Finance before
spending rather than after.

## 2. Submitting an expense claim

1. Submit in **ExpenseHub** within **30 days** of the spend date. Claims older
   than 60 days are rejected except with department-head approval.
2. Attach an itemised receipt for **every claim of USD 25 or more**.
3. Select the correct cost centre and project code.
4. Approval routes to the line manager, then to Finance for claims over
   USD 1,000.
5. Approved claims are paid with the **next payroll run** (see HR-POL-007).

## 3. Approval thresholds

| Amount (per claim) | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to USD 250 | Line manager |
| USD 251 – 1,000 | Line manager + Finance review |
| USD 1,001 – 5,000 | Department head |
| Over USD 5,000 | CFO |

## 4. Travel

- Book through the corporate travel portal. Out-of-portal bookings need prior
  written approval.
- **Air travel:** economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is
  permitted for flights of 6 hours or more. Business class requires CFO
  approval.
- **Rail:** standard class, or first class where it is cheaper than a flexible
  standard fare.
- **Hotels:** up to USD 220 per night in tier-1 cities, USD 150 elsewhere.
- **Ground transport:** public transport or ride-share; car rental needs
  manager approval. Personal car mileage is reimbursed at USD 0.62 per mile.
- Book at least **14 days in advance** where practical.

## 5. Meals and per diem

| Situation | Limit |
|---|---|
| Per diem while travelling (no receipts) | USD 65 per full day |
| Client entertainment (per head) | USD 90, manager pre-approval required |
| Team meals | USD 45 per head, up to 4 times per year |
| Alcohol | Not reimbursable except at approved client events |

## 6. Not reimbursable

Traffic fines and parking tickets, personal entertainment, airline lounge or
seat-selection upgrades, travel insurance bought separately (corporate cover
already applies), spouse or family travel, and personal subscriptions.

## 7. Corporate cards

Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel more than 4 times a year.
Card statements must be reconciled in ExpenseHub within **15 days** of the
statement date. Personal spend on a corporate card must be repaid within
10 days and repeated misuse leads to withdrawal of the card.

## 8. Fraud and misuse

Deliberately false claims are gross misconduct and may lead to dismissal and
recovery of funds. Concerns can be raised anonymously through the whistleblowing
channel described in the Code of Conduct (HR-POL-010).

## 9. Currency and taxes

Claims are reimbursed in the employee's payroll currency using the ExpenseHub
rate on the transaction date. Employees are responsible for declaring any
taxable benefit where local law requires it; Finance provides an annual summary.
