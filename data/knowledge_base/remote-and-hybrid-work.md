# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## 1. Working patterns

Northwind Systems operates three approved patterns:

| Pattern | Definition | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| **Hybrid (default)** | Minimum **2 days per week** in an assigned office | Line manager |
| **Fully remote** | No office attendance requirement | Line manager + department head |
| **Office-based** | 4–5 days per week, for roles requiring on-site presence | Role profile |

Anchor days are agreed per team so that a team is co-located on at least one
common day. Teams publish anchor days in their team charter.

## 2. Core hours and flexibility

Core collaboration hours are **10:00–16:00 in the employee's registered time
zone**. Outside core hours, employees may arrange their day as they wish
provided commitments are met. Meetings should be scheduled inside core hours
and recorded when participants span more than 4 time zones.

## 3. Requesting a change of pattern

Submit **PeopleHub → Working Pattern → Change Request**. The manager must
respond within **20 working days**, in writing, with reasons if declining.
Declines may be appealed to the department head within 10 working days.
Requests are assessed on role suitability, team coverage and performance — not
on personal circumstances.

## 4. Working from another location

- Working from a different location **within your country of employment**
  requires no approval beyond informing your manager.
- Working from **another country** requires approval from HR and Tax at least
  **30 days in advance** and is capped at **30 calendar days per rolling
  12 months** because of tax and permanent-establishment risk.
- Requests to permanently relocate to another country are treated as an
  internal transfer and may change salary band and benefits.

## 5. Home office and equipment

- A one-time home office allowance of **USD 500** is available after probation,
  claimed through the expense system (FIN-POL-006).
- Company laptops remain company property, must run the MDM agent, and must not
  be used by family members.
- Employees must have an internet connection able to sustain video calls. A
  monthly connectivity stipend of **USD 30** applies to fully remote employees.

## 6. Health, safety and insurance

Remote employees complete an annual home workstation self-assessment in
PeopleHub. Northwind Systems' employer liability insurance covers the
designated home workspace during working hours. Employees must report any
work-related injury within 24 hours.

## 7. Meeting and documentation norms

- Default to asynchronous, written decisions. Every recurring meeting needs an
  agenda and a written outcome.
- Decisions are recorded in the team wiki, not only in chat.
- Any meeting with a remote participant is a remote meeting: everyone joins
  with their own camera and microphone.

## 8. Non-compliance

Repeated failure to meet an agreed pattern is a performance matter handled by
the line manager under the Performance Policy (HR-POL-009), starting with an
informal conversation.
