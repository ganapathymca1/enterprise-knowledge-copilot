# New Joiner Onboarding Guide

Onboarding at Northwind Systems runs from offer acceptance to the end of the
first 90 days. It is jointly owned by the **hiring manager** (role context),
**People Operations** (process and paperwork) and the **onboarding buddy**
(day-to-day support).

## 1. Before day one (pre-boarding)

People Operations completes the following at least 5 working days before the
start date:

1. Send the welcome email with start time, location and dress code.
2. Trigger background verification through the HR system.
3. Raise the IT ticket for laptop, accounts and hardware shipment.
4. Assign an onboarding buddy from the same or an adjacent team.
5. Publish the first-week calendar (orientation, team intro, 1:1s).

The new joiner completes their personal details, tax forms and bank details in
**PeopleHub → My Profile** before the start date.

## 2. Day one checklist

- Attend the 09:30 welcome and orientation session (60 minutes, remote-friendly).
- Collect or unbox equipment; complete device enrolment in the MDM agent.
- Set up SSO and register a second factor in the identity portal.
- Complete the **Code of Conduct** acknowledgement in PeopleHub.
- Meet the onboarding buddy for a 30-minute informal introduction.
- Confirm payroll details are correct (see HR-POL-007).

## 3. First week

| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Orientation, accounts, equipment, buddy intro |
| 2 | Security and privacy training (mandatory, 90 minutes) |
| 3 | Team rituals, tooling walkthrough, repository access |
| 4 | Product and domain overview session |
| 5 | First 1:1 with the line manager; agree 30-day objectives |

Mandatory compliance modules — Code of Conduct, Information Security, Data
Privacy, and Anti-Harassment — must all be completed **within the first
10 working days**. Completion is tracked in PeopleHub and reported to the
manager.

## 4. The 30/60/90 plan

- **Day 30 — Learn.** Understand the team's mission, systems and customers.
  Ship one small, low-risk change. Complete all compliance training.
- **Day 60 — Contribute.** Own a scoped piece of work end to end. Participate
  in on-call shadowing if the team runs an on-call rotation.
- **Day 90 — Operate.** Work independently at the expected level, with a formal
  probation review recorded by the manager in PeopleHub.

## 5. Access requests

Standard access (email, chat, code repository, ticketing, wiki) is provisioned
automatically from the role profile. Additional access is requested through
**ServiceDesk → Access Request** and requires manager approval. Production data
access additionally requires a data-owner approval and completion of the data
handling module.

## 6. Probation

The standard probation period is **3 months**. It may be extended once, by up
to 3 months, with a written development plan. Notice during probation is
2 weeks on either side.

## 7. Onboarding feedback

New joiners receive a survey on day 15 and day 75. Results feed the quarterly
onboarding review. Issues that need immediate attention should go to
`people-ops@northwind-example.com` rather than waiting for the survey.

## 8. Manager responsibilities

Hiring managers must: confirm the start date at least 10 working days in
advance, prepare the 30/60/90 plan before day one, hold weekly 1:1s for the
first 12 weeks, and record the probation decision at least 10 working days
before probation ends.
