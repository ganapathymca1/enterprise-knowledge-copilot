# Payroll and Compensation Policy

## 1. Pay cycle

- Salaries are paid **monthly, on the 25th**. If the 25th falls on a weekend or
  public holiday, payment is made on the **preceding working day**.
- Payslips are published in **PeopleHub → Payroll → Payslips** two working days
  before payday and are retained for 7 years.
- Payroll input closes on the **10th of each month**. Changes received after
  cut-off (new starters, leavers, allowances, expense reimbursements) are
  processed in the following month.

## 2. Pay structure

Total compensation consists of:

1. **Base salary** — fixed annual amount, paid monthly.
2. **Performance bonus** — target 10% of base for individual contributors and
   15% for people managers, paid annually in March based on the prior calendar
   year. Pro-rated for partial years; employees must be employed and not under
   notice on the payment date.
3. **Equity** — restricted stock units for eligible roles, vesting over 4 years
   with a 1-year cliff, vesting quarterly thereafter.
4. **Allowances** — on-call, shift, connectivity and location allowances where
   applicable.

## 3. Salary bands and reviews

Every role maps to a level and a published salary band. Bands are reviewed
annually against market data. The **annual compensation review takes effect on
1 April**, based on performance rating, position in band and market movement.
Out-of-cycle increases require department-head and HR approval and are reserved
for promotions, retention risk and role changes.

## 4. On-call allowance

| Rotation | Allowance |
|---|---|
| Weekday on-call (per night) | USD 40 |
| Weekend / public holiday (per day) | USD 120 |

Allowances are claimed automatically from the paging tool rota and appear on
the following month's payslip.

## 5. Overtime

Salaried exempt employees are not eligible for overtime; sustained overtime is a
resourcing issue to raise with the manager. Non-exempt employees are paid at
1.5× the hourly rate for approved hours beyond 40 per week, and 2× on public
holidays. Overtime must be approved in advance.

## 6. Payroll corrections

Report a suspected payroll error to `payroll@northwind-example.com` within
30 days of the payslip date. Underpayments of more than 5% of net pay are
corrected by an off-cycle payment within **5 working days**. Overpayments are
recovered by an agreed repayment plan over a period no shorter than the period
during which the overpayment occurred.

## 7. Deductions and statutory items

Statutory deductions follow the employee's country of employment. Voluntary
deductions (pension top-ups, charitable giving, equipment purchase plans) are
authorised in PeopleHub and can be stopped with one payroll cycle of notice.

## 8. Confidentiality

Compensation information is confidential. Employees may discuss their own pay,
but managers and HR must not disclose another employee's compensation. Payroll
data is accessible only to Payroll, the employee, and the employee's HR
Business Partner.
