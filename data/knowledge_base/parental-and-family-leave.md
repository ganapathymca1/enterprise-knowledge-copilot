# Parental and Family Leave Policy

## 1. Principle

Northwind Systems offers gender-neutral parental leave. Entitlement depends on
whether an employee is the **primary caregiver** or **secondary caregiver**,
not on gender, and applies equally to birth, adoption and surrogacy.

## 2. Entitlement

| Role | Paid leave | Eligibility |
|---|---|---|
| Primary caregiver | 18 weeks at 100% pay | 6 months continuous service |
| Secondary caregiver | 6 weeks at 100% pay | 6 months continuous service |
| Adoption (primary) | 18 weeks at 100% pay | 6 months continuous service |
| Pregnancy loss leave | 2 weeks at 100% pay | No minimum service |
| Fertility treatment | Up to 5 days paid per year | No minimum service |

Employees with less than 6 months of service receive the statutory minimum for
their work location.

## 3. Taking the leave

- Primary caregiver leave may be taken in up to **3 blocks** within 24 months
  of the birth or placement date. Each block must be at least 2 weeks.
- Secondary caregiver leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or
  placement date.
- Annual leave continues to accrue during paid parental leave.
- Public holidays falling inside a leave period are not deducted.

## 4. Notification

Notify your manager and HR at least **8 weeks before** the expected start date
via **PeopleHub → Time Off → Parental Leave**. Supporting documentation (for
example, a maternity certificate or adoption placement letter) is uploaded to
the same request. HR confirms the plan in writing within 10 working days.

## 5. Return to work

- A **phased return** at 80% hours on 100% pay is available for the first
  4 weeks after returning from primary caregiver leave.
- Employees returning from parental leave keep the same role, level and pay.
- A return-to-work conversation with the line manager is scheduled in the first
  week back, covering objectives, working pattern and any adjustments.
- Nursing parents are entitled to reasonable paid breaks and access to a
  private room at every office.

## 6. Caregiver and dependant leave

Employees may take up to **10 paid days per year** to care for a dependant with
a serious health condition. Requests are approved by the line manager and do
not reduce the annual leave balance.

## 7. Flexible working after leave

Requests for a permanent change in working pattern following parental leave are
handled under the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy (HR-POL-005). Managers must
respond within 20 working days and give a written reason if declining.

## 8. Contact

Questions: `people-ops@northwind-example.com`. All parental leave records are
confidential and visible only to the employee, their HR Business Partner and
payroll.
