# Benefits and Wellbeing Policy

## 1. Eligibility

All permanent employees working 20 hours or more per week are eligible from
their **first day of employment**, unless stated otherwise below. Fixed-term
employees on contracts of 6 months or more receive the same core benefits.

## 2. Health cover

- Private medical cover for the employee, with the option to add a partner and
  dependants at employee cost through the annual enrolment window.
- Dental and vision cover included at the standard tier.
- Annual health check reimbursed up to **USD 250**.
- Pre-existing conditions are covered; there is no waiting period for the
  employee's own cover.

## 3. Mental health and Employee Assistance Programme

- **Employee Assistance Programme (EAP):** free, confidential, 24×7 telephone
  and video counselling for employees and household members. Up to **8
  sessions per issue per year** at no cost. Northwind Systems receives only
  anonymised aggregate usage data — never individual names.
- **2 wellbeing days per year**, taken with same-day notice and not deducted
  from annual leave.
- Manager mental-health-awareness training is mandatory for all people managers.

## 4. Retirement

Company pension contribution of **6% of base salary**, matched up to a further
**3%** of employee contribution (maximum 9% employer contribution). Enrolment is
automatic after 30 days; employees may opt out or change contributions monthly
in PeopleHub.

## 5. Insurance

| Cover | Amount |
|---|---|
| Life assurance | 4× base salary |
| Income protection | 65% of base salary after 13 weeks of absence, to age 65 |
| Business travel insurance | Automatic for all approved business travel |
| Personal accident | 2× base salary |

## 6. Salary continuation during long-term illness

| Continuous service | Full pay | Half pay |
|---|---|---|
| Under 1 year | 4 weeks | 4 weeks |
| 1–3 years | 8 weeks | 8 weeks |
| 3+ years | 13 weeks | 13 weeks |

After the periods above, income protection (Section 5) applies.

## 7. Learning and wellbeing allowances

- **Learning allowance:** USD 1,500 per employee per year (see HR-POL-012).
- **Wellbeing allowance:** USD 400 per year for gym membership, sports
  equipment, ergonomic items or mindfulness subscriptions. Claimed through
  ExpenseHub under the `WELLBEING` code.
- **Referral bonus:** USD 2,000 paid after the referred employee completes
  3 months.

## 8. Annual enrolment

The benefits enrolment window runs each year from **1 to 30 November**, with
changes effective 1 January. Outside the window, changes are permitted only
after a qualifying life event (marriage, birth or adoption, bereavement, change
of partner's cover) reported within 30 days of the event.

## 9. Questions

Benefits questions go to `benefits@northwind-example.com`. Claims and cover
details are handled directly by the insurer; Northwind Systems does not see
medical information.
