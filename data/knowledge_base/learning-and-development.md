# Learning and Development Policy

## 1. Learning allowance

Every employee past probation has an annual learning budget of **USD 1,500**
for courses, conferences, books, certifications and subscriptions relevant to
their role or agreed career direction. The budget runs on the calendar year and
**does not carry over**.

Spend is claimed through ExpenseHub under the `LEARNING` code with manager
approval. Items over USD 750 need department-head approval.

## 2. Study and exam leave

Up to **5 days of paid study or exam leave** per year are available for an
approved course or certification, requested in PeopleHub as leave type
`STUDY`. Where the company funds a qualification costing more than USD 3,000,
a repayment agreement applies: 100% if the employee leaves within 6 months of
completion, 50% within 12 months, nothing thereafter.

## 3. Certifications

Cloud, security and data certifications listed on the L&D wiki are fully funded
outside the personal allowance, including one re-sit. Employees are expected to
share learnings in a team session or a written internal post.

## 4. Conferences

One external conference per year is funded per employee, subject to manager
approval and the travel policy (FIN-POL-006). Speakers at approved conferences
have travel funded outside the personal learning budget.

## 5. Internal learning

- **Tech talks** — weekly, recorded, open to all.
- **Mentoring programme** — matched pairs for 6-month cycles, opened twice a
  year.
- **Internal mobility** — employees with 12 months in role may apply for
  internal openings without the current manager's prior approval; HR informs
  the manager once an interview is scheduled.
- **Onboarding academy** — role-specific curriculum for new joiners
  (HR-POL-003).

## 6. Learning time

Employees may use up to **4 hours per month of working time** for structured
learning, agreed with the manager and planned like any other work.

## 7. Measuring outcomes

L&D reports quarterly on budget utilisation, certification completions and
internal mobility. Individual learning records are visible to the employee and
their manager only.
