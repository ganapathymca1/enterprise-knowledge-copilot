# Performance and Career Framework

## 1. Cycle

Northwind Systems runs a **twice-yearly** performance cycle:

| Cycle | Period reviewed | Conversations | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 check-in | Jan – Jun | July | Written feedback, no rating |
| Annual review | Jan – Dec | January – February | Rating, promotion, compensation input |

Ratings feed the compensation review effective 1 April (HR-POL-007).

## 2. Rating scale

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Exceptional** | Consistently exceeded the level's expectations; broad impact |
| **Strong** | Fully met and often exceeded expectations |
| **Solid** | Met expectations for the level |
| **Developing** | Below expectations in one or more areas; support plan agreed |
| **Not meeting** | Substantially below expectations; formal improvement plan |

There is **no forced distribution or stack ranking**. Calibration sessions
across a department exist to align standards, not to fit a curve.

## 3. Inputs

1. Employee self-assessment.
2. Manager assessment against the level expectations in the career framework.
3. **Peer feedback** — 3 to 5 reviewers, chosen by the employee and agreed with
   the manager.
4. Objectives set at the start of the period (outcome-based, not task lists).

## 4. Continuous feedback

Weekly or bi-weekly 1:1s are expected for every employee. Feedback should be
specific, timely and behavioural. There should be **no surprises** at review
time: anything in a formal review must already have been discussed.

## 5. Promotion

Promotion recognises sustained performance **already demonstrated at the next
level**, typically for two consecutive quarters. Cases are written by the
manager, reviewed in department calibration, and approved by the department head
and HR. Promotion decisions are communicated with written feedback, including
for unsuccessful cases, with a development plan.

## 6. Performance improvement

When performance is below expectations, the manager opens an informal support
period of 4–6 weeks with clear, measurable expectations. If it does not
improve, a formal **Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)** of 8 weeks is agreed
with HR, with weekly checkpoints and a written outcome. Employees may bring a
colleague to PIP meetings and may appeal the outcome to HR within 10 working
days.

## 7. Career framework

Levels run from L1 (Associate) to L8 (Distinguished / Director+) on both the
individual-contributor and management tracks. The tracks are of equal standing
and employees may move between them without loss of level. Level expectations
are published in the career framework wiki and cover scope, autonomy, impact,
craft and collaboration.

## 8. Records

Reviews are stored in PeopleHub and visible to the employee, their manager,
the skip-level manager and HR. Employees may add a written comment to any
review they disagree with; the comment becomes part of the record.
