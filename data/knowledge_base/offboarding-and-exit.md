# Offboarding and Exit Policy

## 1. Notice periods

| Level | Employee notice | Company notice |
|---|---|---|
| Probation (any level) | 2 weeks | 2 weeks |
| L1 – L3 | 4 weeks | 4 weeks |
| L4 – L6 | 8 weeks | 8 weeks |
| L7+ | 12 weeks | 12 weeks |

Notice is given in writing to the line manager and copied to HR. The company
may agree a shorter notice period by mutual consent, or pay in lieu of notice.

## 2. Resignation process

1. Employee sends written notice to the manager and HR.
2. HR acknowledges within 2 working days and confirms the last working day.
3. The manager agrees a handover plan within 5 working days, covering owned
   systems, documentation, on-call rota removal and open work.
4. HR schedules an exit interview in the final week.

## 3. Final pay

The final payslip includes salary to the last working day, **payment for
accrued but unused annual leave**, approved outstanding expenses, and any
contractual bonus. Leave taken beyond the accrued entitlement is deducted.
Final pay is issued in the **next scheduled payroll run** after the last
working day. Equity treatment follows the plan rules: unvested units lapse on
the last working day unless the leaver is classified as a good leaver by the
plan administrator.

## 4. Return of company property

All laptops, phones, hardware keys, access cards and other equipment must be
returned by the last working day, either in person or with the prepaid shipping
label provided by IT. Unreturned property may be recovered from final pay where
local law permits.

## 5. Access revocation

All system access is revoked automatically at **18:00 on the last working day**.
Mailbox contents are transferred to the line manager and retained for 12 months
before deletion. Personal files must be removed by the employee before the last
day; IT does not recover personal data after access is revoked.

## 6. Exit interviews

Exit interviews are voluntary and conducted by HR, not the line manager.
Feedback is anonymised and reported quarterly in aggregate. Individual comments
are never shared with the line manager without the leaver's consent.

## 7. Post-employment obligations

Confidentiality obligations survive employment indefinitely. Non-solicitation of
employees and customers applies for 6 months after the last working day where
enforceable locally. References are provided centrally by HR and confirm role,
dates of employment and, with consent, salary.

## 8. Re-hire

Former employees who left in good standing may be re-hired; continuous service
is restored for benefits purposes if the gap is under 12 months. Employees
dismissed for gross misconduct are not eligible for re-hire.

## 9. Redundancy

Where a role is at risk, a consultation period of at least 30 days applies,
with individual meetings, consideration of suitable alternative roles, and a
redundancy payment of **2 weeks of base pay per completed year of service**
(minimum 4 weeks), in addition to notice, or the statutory minimum where that
is greater.
